What's the ideal loadout for civilizational collapse?

I diverge hard from traditional prepping here, because I think it has the wrong goal and, often, a warped perspective.

It's not about just protecting yourself and your family.

It's about protecting humans and human culture - preserving and/or recreating as much of technological society as you think worthwhile.

What do you put in your backpack?

- A copy of _Bootstrapping Civilization_ (constraint for the book[s]; it has to fit in a backpack)
