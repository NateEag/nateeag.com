

## Pocketknife

I have been carrying an older variant of the [Mikov Fixir 232 -
XH-1](https://www.mikov.cz/nuz-232-xh-1-kp/) since I bought it in the late
1990s, probably around '97 or '98.

It has served me very well throughout all manner of mild to moderate use and
abuse. In my youth I put the lock through a lot of heavy strain hacking and
carving through small branches, and it remains rock-solid today.

The knife itself has been lost and returned to me repeatedly, like the night in
my teens I realized I didn't have it after running with friends through a
miles-wide field of waist-high grass (my friend found it in the back of his
car), or the time in my twenties I forgot to leave it home when going to the
airport for a flight (I bailed out of the security line, hid it under a bush
outdoors, and retrieved it none the worse for wear a week later on my return).

At this rate, it may just outlive me - if so, I hope one of my children will
put it to good use.


## Wallet

I used the wallet my grandmother made me for about thirty-five years.

When it was finally so dilapidated that my cards fell through the holes in the
bottom, I replaced it with the [Fantom X
wallet](https://store.fantomwallet.com/fantom-x-1/) with the money clip
attachment.

I am very happy with it and would recommend it without reservation.

I carry eight cards and a small billfold in it. The card-fanning mechanism
makes picking the right one much easier than a standard wallet, and the limited
carrying capacity keeps me from carrying too many cards.

I keep it in my front pocket along with my cell phone (which is itself in a
case).


## Smartphone

I've gone through several smartphones since I finally caved in and started
using one circa 2016.

After a lot of painstaking, obsessive research, I purchased a [Fairphone
5](https://shop.fairphone.com/fairphone-5) in December 2025, in hopes of both
supporting a better way of making electronics and of not having to replace my
phone due to failure every three years.

Most of my research was focused on whether the phone would fulfill basic
telephony functions for me, as Fairphone does not support it in the US. Because
of that I bought it through [Clove
Technologies](https://www.clove-technology.com/products/fairphone-5-5g), after
confirming I had a fourteen-day window in which to return it if I could not get
it to meet my telephony needs.

I was surprised and delighted to discover that here in central PA, at least, it
works very well with [Ting](https://www.ting.com/mobile-offer), my long-time
cell service provider.

I am impacted by a bug whose behavior appears identical to [this
one](https://gitlab.com/LineageOS/issues/android/-/issues/5511), even though
I'm on the stock Android 14 with a Fairphone 5 - after boot, my phone call
audio is not transmitted until I use a microphone other than the default one
during a call. After doing so, the default mic seems to work fine indefinitely
until the next reboot. I didn't notice this bug for the first few days, as I
often use my Bluetooth earbuds for phone calls.
