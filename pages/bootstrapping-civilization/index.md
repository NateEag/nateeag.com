I have spent way more of my life than is reasonable pondering how to restart technological civilization on Earth after a hypothetical collapse.

I would love to write the field manual for doing so, tentatively dubbed _Bootstrapping Civilization_.

There are several existing pieces of prior art, which I have read and enjoyed, but none of which have been what I want.

They're usually high-level abstract plans for how one might do it.

I want the acid-tested, field guide for practically doing it.

Don't just tell me "Here's the chemicals you need to make soap, and the risks you should know about," give me a practical guide to doing it with the tools and techniques I probably have at this point in our restart.

Prior Art:

* The Knowledge
* How To Invent Everything: A Survival Guide For The Stranded Time Traveler
